Our Mission

Every Person Has a Story

At Crossroads Hat Company, we believe every person has a story. A story of struggle. A story of loss. A story of redemption. A story of God's faithfulness through every season of life.

Our mission has never been simply to create hats and apparel. Our mission is to advance God's Kingdom by creating conversation starters that encourage people to share their testimony and point others to Christ.

That is why we are honored to partner with WAR TORN — an upcoming faith-based feature film centered on loss, healing, redemption, and the hope found in God.

Why This Partnership Matters

Rooted in the Same Belief

The message behind WAR TORN closely mirrors the heart of Crossroads. Both are rooted in the belief that God meets us at life's crossroads — even in our darkest moments, He is faithful to guide, restore, and transform us for His glory.

Stories have the power to open hearts, start conversations, and remind people that no one is beyond God's reach.

Our hope is not simply to place a logo on screen, but to support stories that open hearts to the Gospel and point people toward Jesus. Through this partnership, our prayer is that more people will encounter the hope of Christ and be encouraged in their own faith journey.

How Crossroads Is Involved

On Set

Crossroads Hat Company is providing hats featured in the film, along with custom hats and apparel for the production team — worn on location during principal photography in New Mexico.

Behind the Scenes

We will be sharing behind-the-scenes content, interviews, and updates that highlight both the making of the film and the mission behind Crossroads — creating opportunities for meaningful conversations about faith, testimony, and the life-changing power of God's grace.
